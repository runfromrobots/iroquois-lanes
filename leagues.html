Bowling & Leagues

Open bowling, leagues, and everything in between

Whether you're dropping in for a casual game or bowling every week with a league, Iroquois Lanes has a spot for you.

Open Bowling

Walk in any time we're open for a casual game. Great for families, date night, or a spur-of-the-moment outing. Call ahead if you're bringing a large group.

Leagues

Iroquois Lanes hosts bowling leagues throughout the season for adults and youth bowlers. New teams and free agents are always welcome. Call the lanes for current league schedules and openings.

Birthday Parties & Groups

Book the lanes for a birthday party, team outing, or group event. Call ahead to reserve lanes and check availability.

Pro Shop

Our on-site pro shop handles ball drilling and fitting, equipment, and shoe rental, so you're ready to bowl whether you're a first-timer or a league regular.

Rates

Current pricing

Lane rates, shoe rental, league fees, and party/event space pricing change by season and group size. Call (518) 673-4464 for current pricing, or ask about it next time you're in.
