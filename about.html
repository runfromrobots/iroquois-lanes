About Us

The Mohawk Valley's spot for bowling and good food

Iroquois Lanes is a bowling center and restaurant in Canajoharie, NY, serving open bowlers, leagues, and families from around the Mohawk Valley.

Bowling for Everyone

From first-timers to league regulars, our lanes are set up for a good time. Automatic scoring keeps every game moving.

Community Leagues

We host adult and youth leagues throughout the season, and we're always glad to welcome new bowlers and free agents.

Food & Drinks

Our restaurant serves apps, sandwiches, and burgers, so you can grab a bite without leaving the building.

Pro Shop

Need equipment, a shoe rental, or a ball drilled? Our pro shop has you covered.

Come see us

Find us at 192 Erie Blvd in Canajoharie, NY, or follow along on Facebook for league updates and news.
